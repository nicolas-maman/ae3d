### Animated figures in scenes

- `ae3d.figure` (#439, slice 1) makes an animated glTF figure one game
  object: its meshes and bones under the object's group, a clip played by
  name at a speed, looping or not, and scrubbed with `set_time`. Scene
  files record it as the group's `"figure"` record, not its meshes, and
  `figure.attach` makes it again on the group a scene gives back.
  `tests/test_figure.ae`: a walking fox and a bending arm saved and read
  back into the same pose, within 4.5 µrad. The Vulkan renderer takes a
  group (a model with nothing to draw) without trying to upload it.
