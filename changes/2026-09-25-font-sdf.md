### Glyphs in Aether: a TrueType reader and a distance-field atlas

- `ae3d.glyphs` (#449, slice 1) reads a TrueType font from its bytes --
  `head`, `maxp`, `hhea`/`hmtx`, `cmap` formats 4 and 12, `loca`/`glyf`
  simple and compound glyphs, `kern` format 0 -- every read bounds-checked,
  a malformed font refused with the table named. It bakes printable ASCII,
  Latin-1 and the missing glyph into one signed-distance-field atlas (8
  bits, 0.5 the outline, the sign by the non-zero rule TrueType fills by),
  and lays UTF-8 text out as quads at a pixel size, kerned, with newlines.
  A bake is cached under `build/cache/fonts` like the OBJ parse.
- The font is PT Sans Regular (ParaType, OFL 1.1), in `resources/fonts/`
  with its licence beside it.
- `tests/test_font.ae`: at 64 px to the em and a 2.5 px spread, 192 glyphs
  in 1024 x 512, baked in 6.2 ms and read back from the cache in 0.4 ms;
  the 0.5 contour within 0.40 px of the true outline (0.011 px on average
  over 41,658 crossings), each texel's distance within 0.028 px; "Hello,
  world" at 32 px exactly its advances and kerning, 164.224 px; 200
  corrupted copies of the font loaded or refused without a crash.
  [docs/ui.md](docs/ui.md).
